⚙️ Seven-Step End-to-End Operational Workflow

SylvaSense operationalizes remote-sensing forest intelligence through a 7-step pipeline designed to streamline MRV workflows, reduce uncertainties, and provide audit-grade evidence for carbon project developers and validation bodies.

🧠 AI Dual-Branch Feature Fusion Architecture

Feature-level fusion combines optical and radar representations inside the model rather than simply averaging two separate predictions — improving robustness under cloud cover and in structurally complex forests.

🛰️ Branch 1: Optical Encoder

Processes Sentinel-2 reflectance, vegetation indices (NDVI, EVI, NDRE), and canopy condition.

• B2, B3, B4 (RGB Bands)
• B8 NIR & B11 SWIR
• NDVI, EVI, NDRE Indices
📡 Branch 2: SAR Encoder

Processes Sentinel-1 C-band backscatter, polarization ratios (VH/VV), and temporal structural change.

• C-band VV & VH (dB)
• Polarization Ratio VH/VV
• RFDI Degradation Metric
⚡ Fusion Layer

Combines learned representations from both branches into a joint latent feature space.

• Dynamic Cloud Weighting
• Cross-Modal Embeddings
• Joint Feature Tensor
🎯 Prediction Heads

Dedicated heads outputting above-ground biomass, forest density proxy, and uncertainty intervals.

• AGBD (Mg/ha & tCO₂e)
• Stand Density Proxy
• [P10, P50, P90] Bounds

⚖️ Honest Scientific Limitations & Operational Boundaries

As outlined in the core system principles, SylvaSense maintains explicit uncertainty transparency and does not make unsubstantiated claims.

⚠️ Reference Data Quality (GEDI)

NASA GEDI is sampled waveform LiDAR, serving as calibration reference rather than wall-to-wall ground truth. Accuracy depends on coverage in the target area.

⚠️ Dense High-Biomass Saturation

Radar-based biomass estimation can saturate in very dense tropical forests (>275-300 Mg/ha) — uncertainty intervals are appropriately wider in these conditions.

⚠️ Stand-Level Density vs Tree Counting

Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 at 10-meter resolution are not sufficient for reliable individual-tree enumeration. MVP presents stand-level density proxies.

⚠️ Field Validation

Field measurements remain important for local calibration and independent verification. SylvaSense reduces — but does not eliminate — the need for ground surveys.
